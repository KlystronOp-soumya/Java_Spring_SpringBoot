5.start the service
kubectl expose deployment todoappdeployment-74c6ccfb4f-dnc8q --port=8093 --target-port=8093 --type=NodePort --name=todoappcontainer


## Issue why NodePort is not accessible
When using the Docker driver, Minikube runs inside a container, and unfortunately NodePort services aren't directly accessible from your host machine. That’s why even though everything's wired up correctly inside Kubernetes, the outside world can’t reach it via http://<minikube-ip>:<NodePort>.
